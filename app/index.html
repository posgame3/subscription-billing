How it works
Three steps. Fully on-chain.
No backend. No database. Just Solana PDAs and cryptographic guarantees.
1

Connect Wallet

Connect your Phantom wallet. Your public key becomes your identity — no accounts, no passwords, no email required.

2

Choose a Plan

Plans are stored as Program Derived Addresses on Solana. Price, period, and capacity are enforced by smart contract — immutable once set.

3

Subscribe On-Chain

One atomic transaction transfers SOL and creates your Subscription PDA. Every payment is an immutable PaymentRecord — a permanent, auditable on-chain receipt.
